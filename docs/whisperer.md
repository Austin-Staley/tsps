# The Whisperer

`plugins/bosses/Whisperer.plugin.js` owns a post-quest normal encounter. The DT2 quest's own scene and progress are unchanged. Depends on PR #882 for conditional loot and actual-damage observers. Awakened is deliberately unavailable: no orb prompt or consumption occurs.

The public normal Whisperer spawn is replaced at load with the cache's Odd Figure. Disturb requires completed Desert Treasure II and a real Blackstone fragment; the bank placeholder does not qualify. Ketla replaces a lost plain fragment through her existing generated reclaim conversation, granting it at the handout step only when inventory space is available. A PrivateArea isolates the cathedral and its Shadow Realm mirror per player. Tentacle exit/quick-escape, teleport, logout and death clean the session; login recovery returns a saved occupant outside. Graves relocate to the Camdozaal rope, with a 0.25 fee multiplier before five normal kills through the shared grave contract.

The ordinary postquest route is handled independently of the quest's stage-only transport hooks: Camdozaal's rope descends into Lassar, activated public teleporters retain discovered destinations, and the Lassar rope returns beside the Camdozaal sinkhole. New discoveries use `whisperer:teleporters`; existing quest varbits are read without altering quest progress. Cache collision checks choose clear adjacent arrival tiles rather than landing on rope/teleporter objects. The grave and rope return use (2922,5826), the clear southern approach; (2922,5827) is the blocked rock anchor. PB timing freezes at lethal damage, before the delayed death/loot event.

Normal mechanics include 900 HP, three threshold specials (720/495/270), progressively mixed prayer volleys with a separate alternating animation per projectile, fixed flight cycles 30–90 and prayers checked at scheduled impact, moving tentacle paths, mirrored double melee hits, binding pursuit countered with ice/standard binding spells, sanity/prayer drain, insanity damage, 140 HP enrage, loot grading, kill count, PB and a 30-tick Odd Figure respawn. Actual HP loss drives perfect loot; queued encounter damage is cancelled on final death or cleanup. Souls count at lethal damage before delayed NPC death callbacks. The special damage reduction is 70% outside / 60% inside the Shadow Realm. Thrall damage uses the core's direct-hit path and therefore bypasses this PendingHit modifier.

Corrupted seeds use all four Wiki diagram patterns (24 or 28 seeds), shown with light/dark cache locs in the Shadow Realm and uniform locs outside. Screech uses eight columns, with one 60-HP column, one to three 40-HP columns and the rest 20 HP; cover is the two-by-two immediately behind a column, and every blast damages every surviving column. Soul Siphon has 12 one-HP souls in the Wiki's 2/3/3/4 sets, with healing, prayer, sanity and boss-damage rewards; souls are vulnerable only in the Shadow Realm.

## Verification limits

This is not a verified 1:1 implementation. Absolute seed anchoring/orientation, eight-column placement and the distribution beyond published health constraints are inferred from diagrams/video. Special animation timings, seed duration and binding flight timing need a packet capture. Normal melee's 28–42 roll and 75% protected share are provisional; the obtained source confirms max 42, mirrored double hits and partial protection but does not establish the complete distribution. Realm switching is permitted during specials; fragment active/plain transitions are captured, but the exact special deactivation window is not reproduced tick for tick. The escalating zero-sanity damage formula is also a provisional fallback, not a verified normal-mode formula. No in-game client smoke run has been performed.

Awakened geometry, timing, full five-projectile patterns, stat/slayer immunity integration, prayer/sanity changes, special rotations and final-phase mechanics are not implemented. No Awakened CA is awarded. Only kill-count/PB CA contracts are supplied; other CAs require full tracking before being enabled.

Focused tests use real rev241 cache configs, Player/NPC instances, equipment/inventory, HitDamage/HitQueue, skill levels, private areas and source hooks. Rendering/scheduling and deterministic accuracy are doubled only where the unit under test requires it. Build with `node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --build` and run `node --test tests/whisperer.test.cjs` from `server/`.

Review verification: the focused suite passes 30/30, including real cache identity/action checks, encoded projectile cycles/heights/source centre, intro ordering/cancellation, fragment transitions, generated Ketla handout integration and bind/message feedback. Fresh TypeScript build passed. The complete server suite passed 4,978/4,978 tests across 370 files with four workers outside the sandbox, with zero failures or skips. Independent review found no actionable issues and separately reran the 30 focused tests.
