### Current smoke wave: eligible cover and permanent collision

The current [Wiki quest guide's Leviathan special-attack table](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Desert_Treasure_II_-_The_Fallen_Empire#Laying-off_the_Leviathan), re-fetched24October2026, distinguishes the ten-boulder rain from the subsequent smoke blast. It restricts shelter to boulders launched during the preceding attack and explicitly excludes regular boulders. The [normal strategy's smoke description](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Leviathan/Strategies) likewise links shelter to debris left by its ten throws. This is an eligibility rule, distinct from the fact that all permanent rubble blocks player movement. In the2024normal footage around4:15.8–4:23.4, the player shelters behind the row created during the current rain. That supplies positive ordinary-mode corroboration; the negative roar/older-wave cases are Wiki-intent evidence, not claimed packet observations.

Each smoke rain clears the eligible set; successful new permanent smoke-rock placements enter it when they land, while roar rubble and older smoke rocks retain their existing objects/clipping. The blast tests this eligible set. The former implementation accepted every permanent blocker, allowing players to shelter behind unrelated roar rubble or a previous smoke wave. Disabled awakened cover behavior remains unchanged.

Two regressions reproduce actual normal smoke waves and put either a seven-tick roar rock or an earlier smoke-wave rock on the boss-to-player ray. Both failed before correction with99HP rather than40HP, then pass with the intended59damage and persistent movement clipping.
