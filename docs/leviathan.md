# Leviathan encounter design

The post-quest Leviathan is a members-only owner-scoped encounter, entered through the Scar's rowboat and arena handholds after Desert Treasure II. The existing quest encounter remains untouched. Its 7x8 cache-template layout (chunks257,792, planes0 and1) supplies the bridge-correct arena, not a behavioral oracle for its simplified attacks.

Sources: [Leviathan](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Leviathan), [Strategies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Leviathan/Strategies), [Perfect Leviathan](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Perfect_Leviathan); local revision241 cache gamevals are authoritative for NPC, loc, sequence, projectile, graphic and varp IDs. Public RSProx NPC12214 search found recordings555,550,100,1750,1754,1751 (LeaguesV) and3285,788 (GridMaster). Their modified stats/loot are not normal or awakened balance evidence. Another private server is not used as an oracle.

Normal mechanics: 900HP; impact-prayer orbs (magic32, ranged24, melee50), adjacent bite50, seven progressively faster volley tiers, avoidable debris and unavoidable roar chip; shadow spells always stun outside enrage, front hits capped10, ranged rear punishment and doubled magic rear damage; alternating lightning and smoke specials, with33% damage reduction during specials;20% enrage with a clockwise moving5x5 pathfinder buff, ranged perfect accuracy/minimum20%-max hit, doubled magic and prayer piercing outside its aura. Stuns do not stop enrage. Smoke already in progress must finish on enrage. Perfect grades exclude roar chip and freeze at lethal boss damage, using actual applied player damage through shared prerequisite#882.

Awakened extensions use2700HP, stronger canonical stats, shortened stun, five-block cross debris, one-tick enrage,7x7 aura and a pursuing tornado below50%HP. The stat-drain immunity foundation is coordinated with the parent task. Uncaptured cadence/visual offsets must be marked as such, rather than described as measured fidelity. No normal/awakened parity claim will be made while critical mechanics are missing.

Lifecycle: reject incomplete quests; consume an Awakener's orb only after a current-state confirmation; create one private session per owner; reset fights on respawn; clean all hazards/NPCs on exit, teleport, death and logout; recover persisted inside flags to a public Scar tile. Death items follow normal gravestones outside by the rowboat. Reduced fees before five normal kills require a shared gravestone provenance modifier, coordinated with the parent task rather than patched locally. Counts and PBs are separate for normal/awakened, persisted, and reported through existing achievement hooks. Canonical NpcDrops owns loot; this plugin only answers context and accessible loot placement. No copied drop table or manual generated-data edits.

Plugin units: Common (API/cache/data/damage), Model (geometry and deterministic volley state), Site (access/session/instance), Fight (lifecycle/hits/stuns/enrage), Hazards (debris/lightning/smoke/pathfinder/tornado), Rewards (counts/PBs/achievement/context/loot location). All content remains under bosses/leviathan with authored balance/timing data under bosses/data.
